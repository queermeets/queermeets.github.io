About Us

Queer Meets started because I was hosting craft nights with my friends and thought, “Why not open this up?” It felt like there were so many parties but not enough chill spaces for sapphics to hang out and make stuff together. So I decided to create one - a casual, creative, and community-focused space for anyone who just wants to vibe, make art, and friends in Toronto

This could not have happened without the immense amount of help and support from my team and friends, so a special shout out to each and every one of them
